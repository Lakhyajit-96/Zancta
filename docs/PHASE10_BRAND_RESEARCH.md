## 6. Search Landscape (Lens B findings F1–F4)

- **F1 — Brandable beats keyword for new entrants.** Google's John Mueller has publicly advised choosing distinctive brand names over generic keyword domains; brandable names are easier to trademark and avoid entity confusion (Search Engine Journal recap of Mueller guidance; NameMesh brandable-vs-keyword guide; wisedigitalpartners.com "SEO Risks of Generic Business Names"). EMD/keyword names still help marginally for geo/transactional queries but are not guaranteed wins (Search Engine Land "Do exact match domains have value in 2025?"; seoexamples.com). For a new brand wanting to own its SERP within months, a unique invented/rare word is the fastest path.
- **F2 — Naming patterns dominating the file-tools space are adjective+keyword or cute-keyword:** iLovePDF, Smallpdf, TinyPNG, TinyWow, PDFgear, Sejda, UPDF, PDF24, Squoosh, Photopea (smallpdf.com/pdf-tools; updf.com iLovePDF-alternatives; softwareworld.co TinyWow alternatives; github awesome-free-browser-tools list). Nearly all are upload-based and compete on "free + many tools."
- **F3 — The privacy niche is active but poorly branded.** "Files never leave your device" is now a recognized category claim (Medium "Privacy-First Web Tools", HackerNoon "Privacy Is Broken in Everyday Tools"). Existing privacy-first entrants found: brevio, KwikPDF, varundeva/privacy-first, Firefox's built-in PDF merge. All are indie, phonetically weak, or keyword-y — the premium/dark/branded lane is open.
- **F4 — Voice/radio test is a real filter.** Standard guidance: name must be spellable after hearing it once, no homophones, no deliberate misspellings (get.tech "radio test"; domaindetails.com voice-search guide; payproglobal.com SaaS naming steps; sparktoro.com 7 rules).

**Implication:** differentiate on (a) a single premium coined/rare word vs. adjective+keyword clutter, and (b) privacy semantics — while avoiding names already owned by password-manager/security/crypto brands, which cluster semantically near us. The semantic territory (privacy/vault/night) is heavily pre-claimed by security, crypto, and password-manager brands — that's why most vault/protect candidates tested RED. The winning strategy is a unique coined or fiction-rooted word (Teneb model), with brand SERP capture driven by consistent entity markup + privacy-tool content, not by keyword match.

## 8. Pronunciation and Memorability (phonetic analysis of finalists)

**Finalists carried into the merged shortlist and their phonetic profiles (Lens A), plus the recommended winner:**

- **Umbra** — UM-bruh. Associations: shadow, eclipse, concealment; maps to privacy ("your files stay in the shadow of your device"). Trochaic, open stressed vowel /ʌ/, soft sonorant cluster /mbr/ — bouba profile, reads elegant and safe. Weakness: no hard consonant ending, can feel diffuse. Global: Italian/Spanish cognate "ombra"; no negative meanings found. Mem 4 / Premium 5.
- **Sable** — SAY-bul. Heraldic black + sable fur (opulence). Sibilant onset + /bəl/ liquid ending is smooth in most languages. Weakness: slight risk of mishearing as "stable". French "sable" = sand; Spanish "sable" = sabre. Mem 5 / Premium 5.
- **Noctra** — NOK-truh. Nocturnal, night-mode semantics. Initial stop /k/ inside /kt/ cluster gives technical edge; feminine -a ending keeps it brandable. Highly ownable coinage; needs brand-building to carry semantics. Mem 4 / Premium 4.
- **Vesper** — VES-per. Evening/dusk sophistication (also the Vesper martini). Crisp /v/ onset, balanced /sp/ cluster. German "Vesper" = light afternoon snack. Mem 4 / Premium 5.
- **Sigil** — SIJ-il. Seal/mark of authenticity, occult-premium vibe. Weakness: pronunciation ambiguity (/ˈsɪdʒɪl/ vs lay "SIG-il"). Mem 4 / Premium 4.
- **Teneb** — TEN-eb. Darkness/concealment (tenebrous). Radio-safe spelling; no tech owner of the word; downside is residual MTG-card noise and the word needs one exposure to teach pronunciation. Harsher phonetics, 5 letters.
- **Privane** — private + arcane; carries "private" audibly without being the keyword; premium, almost archival feel. Near-collision with Charlie Parker jazz tune "Au Privave".
- **Noctum** — night semantics; strongest dark-premium aesthetic among Lens B's set; mood rather than privacy, so positioning must supply the privacy story.
- **ZANCTA (recommended)** — pronounceable **ZANK-ta**. Coined from *sanctum/sanctuary* semantics — a safe place for files. The sharp /k/ stop reads precise/technical while the open -a ending keeps it elegant and brandable; two syllables, spelling–sound transparent, passes the radio test (spellable after hearing once).

**Phonetic framework applied:** syllable/letter economy; trochaic initial stress aids recall; sibilants/fricatives (s, v, f) read smooth/fast, stops (k, t, p) precise/technical, sonorants (m, n, l, r) premium/elegant; bouba–kiki effect; spelling–sound transparency for non-native speakers.

## 9. SEO Considerations

- **Brandable > keyword** for a new entrant (F1): a unique coined/rare word is the fastest path to owning the SERP within months; also easier to trademark and avoids entity confusion.
- **Category clutter is adjective+keyword** (F2: iLovePDF, Smallpdf, TinyPNG…): a single premium coined word differentiates immediately.
- **The privacy lane is open for a real brand** (F3): existing privacy-first entrants are indie/keyword-y; premium/dark/branded positioning is uncontested.
- **Radio/voice test** (F4): the name must be spellable after hearing it once — favors coined words with transparent spelling.
- **Per-candidate SERP ownability:** Teneb GREEN (no tech owner; MTG noise to outgrow); Privane YELLOW-GREEN (buyable .com signal, but privane.dev adjacent project + jazz-tune noise); Noctum YELLOW at first pass but upgraded RED after trademark finding. ZANCTA: zero discoverable footprint → brand SERP fully ownable from day one, with entity markup + privacy-tool content driving capture.
- Domain availability is an SEO reality: **11 of 12 merged-shortlist `.com`s are registered**; exact-match `.com` acquisition would require aftermarket spend.
